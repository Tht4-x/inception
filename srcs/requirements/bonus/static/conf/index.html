ABOUT US

Founded in 2013 in Mulhouse, Dancel Flour Co. started as a small mill on the banks of the Rhine. Today we operate one of the most modern milling facilities in the Grand Est region.

Our wheat is sourced exclusively from local Alsatian farms within 50km of our mill. Every batch is tested for protein content, moisture and purity before milling.

We supply bakeries, pastry shops, restaurants and industrial producers across 12 countries — always with the same commitment to quality.
